During Your Stay

Find instructions for the pool & spa, speakers, and elevator below. Any other questions? Reach out to concierge or Carolina Breeze Vacations.

Trash & Recycling
Roll bins out Monday evening for Tuesday morning pickup.
Pool & Spa Cleanliness
Rinse off at the outdoor shower before entering the house, pool, or spa.
Air Conditioning
Each floor has its own thermostat. Do not set below 72°F. Keep exterior doors closed.
Safety
Swimming
No lifeguards. Check tide & flag conditions. Rip currents near inlets — swim parallel to shore if caught, not against it.
Check conditions
Turtle Nesting (May–Oct)
Fill in holes. Remove gear from sand at night. Turn off beach lights — hatchlings follow light.
Medical
Hospital: Novant Health Brunswick (Bolivia) ~25 min | Urgent care: Shallotte ~15 min

How-To Guides

Pool & Spa
Hayward system with the spa preset to 99°F.
Heating cycle runs approximately one hour.
View InstructionsHide Instructions

Heating the Spa

1Select Spa on the control panel.
2No temperature adjustment needed — preset to 99°F.
3Wait 15 minutes for the spa to heat.
4Leave settings as programmed — don't switch modes.

Jets & Blower

Activate automatically when heating. Can also turn on manually with separate controls if desired.

After 1 Hour

The system automatically returns to "Pool mode." The pool may feel warmer during this time due to spillover from the spa — this is normal.

Pool Lights

The pool lights come on automatically from 7 p.m. to 12 a.m.

Music & Audio (Indoor & Outdoor)
Sonos speaker system. Connect once, then choose where to listen.
View InstructionsHide Instructions

Sonos App (iPhone / Android)

1Connect to Witt's End Wi-Fi (password: Bythesea)
2Open the Sonos app (or download it)
3Tap Add if prompted. Speaker will flash green.
4Pick your room and select your music.

AirPlay (iPhone / iPad)

1Connect to Witt's End Wi-Fi
2Open Control Center → tap audio icon
3Select Sonos speaker → press play

Outdoor TV Audio

Turn on the outdoor TV → open Sound settings → select speakers as audio output. Do not reset or reconfigure speakers.

The Elevator
Private service elevator connecting ground level to all three floors. Close the gate before traveling.
View InstructionsHide Instructions

Using the Elevator

1Check all doors and gates are fully closed on every floor.
2Press the Call button and wait for arrival.
3Close the gate completely before selecting your floor.
4If the elevator doesn't respond, check that no door/gate is open on another floor.

Tip: If the elevator is on another floor, go there first, enter, and ride it to your desired floor.
